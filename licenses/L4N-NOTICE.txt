The original readme_l4n.txt is included unchanged. The reference
left4dead2/neko/config_template.vdf is merged into the active config.vdf
using the previous template and maintainer's preset, preserving its existing
settings while incorporating the new template changes. The template itself
is the sole original file not shipped separately. All other original files,
including bin/neko tools, SDK, nested archive and launchers, are included.
The maintainer's integration preset additionally supplies dxvk.conf and
left4dead2/shaders/fxc/neko_floattoscreen_ps20b.vcs.
These additions and the configuration merge are identified separately in
the repository's runtime/l4n/manifest.json and must not be
represented as the original L4N defaults.

This repository builds the Bridge Client/Hosts and the separate optional
L4D2BridgePlugin.dll. Packaging them with L4N does not transfer authorship of
L4N to this repository or imply endorsement by its original author.

L4N-Necola compatibility extension (necola 1.4.1)

The maintainer supplied the completed L4N-Necola package. This bundle takes
its l4n_necola_bridge.dll plugin, the unchanged original necola.dll,
integration settings and ten converted audio-library VPKs. It preserves
L4N 2.53.0 and loads necola through L4N's plugin interface; this is not a
transfer of necola authorship or a claim that its original source was merged.
necola and its original audio-library content remain the work of their
original rightsholders. The supplied necola archive contains no separate
license grant; this repository's MIT license does not relicense that content.

The integration plugin and configuration are distinct from the original
L4N and necola components. Audio scripts use L4N loading paths; the supplied
conversion repairs missing closing braces in nine supplemental scripts.
Their original caches and base script content remain intact. These are
sound mappings and caches, not additional WAV, PCF or weapon-model assets.
The standalone necola launcher, merger documentation, file manifests,
install/restore scripts and diagnostic material are not included.
